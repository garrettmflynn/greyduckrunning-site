About the show

Grey Duck Running is a podcast about endurance events in the Upper Midwest. Christian and Lauren recap races and rides they've done, look ahead to ones coming up, and talk through the practical side of an event — the course, the logistics, what the day was actually like.

It's made for regular runners rather than competitive ones. Episodes run about an hour.

  • Running races 5Ks, halfs and fulls, and trail races around the region.
  • Bike events Gravel rides and long-distance events like Ride Across Wisconsin.
  • Hiking Trails and long days out across the Upper Midwest.
  • Previews and recaps What a course is like and whether it's worth signing up for.
