About the Show

Grey Duck Running is a podcast about endurance events in the Upper Midwest. Christian and Lauren recap races and rides they've done, look ahead to ones coming up, and talk through the practical side of an event — the course, the logistics, what the day was actually like.

  • Running Races

    5Ks, halfs and fulls, and trail races around the region.

  • Bike Events

    Gravel rides and long-distance events like Ride Across Wisconsin.

  • Hiking

    Trails and long days out across the Upper Midwest.

  • Previews and Recaps

    What a course is like and whether it's worth signing up for.
