About the Show

Grey Duck Running is a podcast about endurance events in the Upper Midwest. Christian and Lauren recap races and rides they've done, and preview ones coming up.

  • Running Races

    5Ks, half and full marathons, and trail races around the region.

  • Bike Events

    Gravel rides and long-distance events like Ride Across Wisconsin.

  • Hiking

    Trails across the Upper Midwest.

  • Previews and Recaps

    What a course is like and whether it's worth signing up for.
